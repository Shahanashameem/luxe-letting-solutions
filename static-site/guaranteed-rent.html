Guaranteed Rent

A fixed monthly income, contracted for the full term

We agree a figure at the outset and pay it on the same date every month — whether the property is occupied, between occupants or undergoing works.

What the agreement covers

  • Fixed monthly payment, occupied or not
  • Rent collection and arrears risk
  • Void periods across the full term
  • Routine cleaning and turnovers
  • Day-to-day maintenance coordination
  • Scheduled inspections and reporting

What remains with you

  • Mortgage payments and ground rent
  • Buildings insurance
  • Structural repairs and major works
  • Statutory landlord obligations

The precise split is stated in your agreement before signature.

How It Works

Six steps from first call to guaranteed rent

A defined process with no ambiguity about what happens next, or when.

  1. 01

    Property Assessment

    We review the property, location and specification, then confirm the achievable guaranteed figure.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    A written proposal setting out the monthly rent, term length and exactly what we take responsibility for.

  3. 03

    Agreement

    A clear company let agreement is signed, with terms reviewed by your solicitor or agent if you prefer.

  4. 04

    Property Preparation

    Compliance checks, inventory, furnishing and photography are completed before occupancy begins.

  5. 05

    Professional Management

    Occupancy, cleaning, inspections and maintenance run day to day under our operations team.

  6. 06

    Guaranteed Rent

    Your fixed monthly payment arrives on the same date each month for the full length of the term.
